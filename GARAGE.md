# The garage — criteria, shortlist, and inspection notes

TJ's own notes, captured verbatim in substance. This is the source of truth for what to look
for and what to walk away from. Prices and mileages are TJ's targets, not market quotes;
validate every one with `marketcheck-dealer-deal-finder` before acting on it.

---

## Must-haves (any daily driver)

- Remote start
- Head-up display
- Blind spot monitoring
- Trunk space

## Sourcing rules

- **Buy from southern states.** Less corrosion means less to fix and better resale.
- **Avoid snowy states** (road salt) **and coastal states** (salt air).
- **Carfax: fewer than 4 owners.** More than that is a walk.

## Known problems by car

### 2019 Cadillac CTS-V
- Blow-by from the blower
- Oil consumption
- Transmission
- Sunroof metal crunching
- **Mod:** SW cat-back exhaust to factory manifolds

### Camaro ZL1
- Squeaky brakes
- Oil pumps

### McLaren 650S
- Coolant leaks
- Keep the key fob away from the car or it drains the battery
- Turbo inlet — aftermarket replacement
- Thermostat failure
- Battery charger must be a C-Tek

### McLaren (general ownership cost)
- **Biggest expense is paint** — corrosion or correction. Their color codes are hard to match
- Wheel and tire alignment: $5k–$10k
- Oil changes
- Parking garages are hard
- **Steel brakes are cheaper — $3k to replace vs carbon ceramics**
- Touring seats
- Check service history; pay a McLaren specialist ~$1k for an inspection
- McLaren warranty ~$3.5k/year — watch the limits on claims, won't cover brakes
- Ceramic coating
- Plug in the battery tender if leaving it a week or more. Don't leave things plugged in otherwise

### Lamborghini Gallardo
- Coolant leaks
- Oil leaks
- Rear main seal and clutch
- Antifreeze
- Engine light / misfire from spark plugs
- Exhaust system
- **Have a great mechanic**

### Audi RS Q8
- Easy to tune: replace the supercharger pulley and crank pulley, stronger transmission
- PCV valve
- Water pump at 90k miles

### V8 / V10 big-ticket items
- AC compressors: $5k
- Magnetic ride suspension leaks: $2k

### V12 — 2015-or-later Aston Martin (V12 Vantage, Rapide S, Vanquish)
- Peeling leather
- Make sure it has a lifted underside
- Peeling body panels
- Standing droplets
- Large chips; check in bright sunlight for swirls and peeling paint
- Make sure everything opens **and stays open** with struts
- Ask where maintenance was done and how often
- Make sure the keys work
- ~1,000 miles/year — did it sit in the garage most of the time, or has it been driven?
- Has the clutch been replaced? Check every gear
- Make sure the gas flap opens
- Make sure the PRDN buttons don't collapse

## Jaguar XJ / XJL supercharged — full inspection

Trim ladder: Premium Luxury → **Portfolio** (high trim) → **XJR575** (highest).
2016 XJ supercharged, ~80k miles. Around $12k, but **would need to buy a warranty**.

**Known issues**
- Plastic cooling system — the Y-pipe and crossover pipe. Have they been replaced, or is aluminum
  fitted? Due around 60k miles
- Supercharger isolator — ~$1k job
- Air ride vs steel coil springs on the struts — ~$1,500
- Round, oversized steering wheel; outdated tech; electrical problems

**Questions to ask on the lot**
- Is anyone else test driving this car?
- What's the history on this vehicle?
- Why did the previous owner sell?
- How long has it been on your lot?
- Check the infotainment thoroughly, play around with it, and test the audio system
- When was the battery replaced? **When were the two batteries replaced?** More than 3 years → $600
- Any vibrations during the drive → bushing or linkage
- When was the gearbox serviced? **Don't mention the gearbox — ask lower, then find it**
- When was the timing chain replaced? Under warranty? Any chain rattle?
- Tires — $800. "OK, so you can't rotate them, right?"
- Has the front flange been replaced? Has the crossover / rear pipe been replaced? **Gotcha: engine killer**
- $1,200 tune-up for spark plugs
- Supercharger rattle on start and shutdown — $1,000
- Ensure it idles smoothly or the fuel pump needs replacing; ensure it doesn't hiccup on acceleration
- Test speakers for buzzing — $400
- Air suspension rear — ask if the rear suspension has been replaced

**Target build once bought**
- Get all issues addressed
- Smaller pulleys → 650 hp
- CarPlay infotainment upgrade
- X-pipe
- Decat (downpipes)

**Music test** (for the audio system, on every test drive)
- Shabba instrumental
- December 31st
- Back to Back

**Negotiation ladder for the XJ**
- Start at $27k, work down to $25k
- $21k offered for a $23k final
- British Green
